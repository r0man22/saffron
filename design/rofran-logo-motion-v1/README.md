# ROFRAN logo motion concepts

Three concept storyboards generated using the built-in GPT Image tool. Each board contains 12 keyframes. These are visual direction studies for review.

1. **01-threads-to-crocus.png:** logo 1 → logo 3. Three saffron threads unfold into the crocus outline.
2. **02-crocus-to-monogram.png:** logo 3 → logo 2. Petal contours fold into the serif R monogram.
3. **03-monogram-to-threads.png:** logo 2 → logo 1. The R uncoils into three saffron filaments.

## Prompt briefs

Shared direction: logo-brand; 4 columns by 3 rows; exactly 12 numbered keyframes; champagne gold #E5D4A4 on deep burgundy #241416; crisp vector-like graphic shapes; a stationary serif ROFRAN wordmark under each emblem. Use the approved comparison board for the exact logo endpoints and the first approved motion board for presentation style. Continuous changes of existing ribbon geometry, no whole-logo crossfades, no glow, particles or 3D.

**1 → 3:** Keep the original three filaments at the centre. Contours unfurl from their common base into left, centre and right pointed petals. Progressively close and balance the outlines, finishing with the original crocus emblem.

**3 → 2:** Fold the centre petal into the upper bowl of the R, straighten the left petal into its upright and unfold the right petal into its diagonal leg. Gather the interior threads into the retained saffron flourish. Settle into the original serif R.

**2 → 1:** Narrow the R upright, uncoil its upper bowl and lift its diagonal leg into three separate upward filaments. Gather their lower ends into one pointed base, ending with exactly three saffron tips matching logo 1.
